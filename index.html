About Caracol

Caracol is an ancient Maya city in the Cayo District of Belize, located within the Chiquibul Forest Reserve. It is the largest archaeological reserve in Belize. Its most famous structure is Caana, meaning “Sky Place,” which rises about 139 feet and is the tallest man-made structure in Belize.

The site contains plazas, pyramids, ball courts, carved monuments, hieroglyphic inscriptions and remains of a large ancient settlement. The surrounding forest also makes the visit an opportunity to experience Belize's natural environment.

Things to See and Do

  • Explore the Caana “Sky Place” complex.
  • Walk through the ancient plazas and structures.
  • See Maya monuments, altars and carved inscriptions.
  • Visit the ball courts and archaeological areas.
  • Learn about Maya history and archaeology at the visitor facilities.
  • Enjoy the surrounding Chiquibul Forest landscape.
  • Take photographs of the ruins and jungle scenery.
